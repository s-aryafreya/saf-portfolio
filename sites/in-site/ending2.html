The dangers of the magical world prove to be too much. Lost in the dark forest, with no one to help you, the creatures that roam the night close in. You run and hide, but the shadows consume you. Without allies or direction, you are swallowed by the darkness, never to be seen again, your fate lost to the unforgiving fantasy world.
