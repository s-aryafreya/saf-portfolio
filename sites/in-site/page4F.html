Entering the Fantasy World

Returning to Human Life

Home Author's Page Target Audience

After leaving the fae behind, you find yourself back in the human world, but things don’t feel the same. The colors seem duller, the sounds quieter, and the mundane routine of everyday life begins to weigh on you. The memories of the fae’s magic linger in your mind, haunting you like a distant dream. Every time you close your eyes, you feel the pull of their world, calling you back to the freedom and wonder you once felt. But you’ve made your choice, and now you must find a way to live in a world that no longer feels like home. You try to blend back into your human life, but the magic you experienced has left its mark on you forever. You move through your days like a shadow, knowing that, although you’ve left the fae behind, their world will always be a part of you.

Live as a human, but never forget the magical world
