Running from the Vampire

You sprint through the woods, your heart pounding as the vampire’s cold eyes seem to follow your every step. The forest is dark and unfamiliar, the shadows closing in from every direction. You hear the howls of werewolves in the distance, but it’s the silence that frightens you most. You feel the cold air pressing against your skin, but there's no warmth or light in sight. Exhausted, you wonder if hiding from everyone is your best option.[cite: 5]
