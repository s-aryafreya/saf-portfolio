The werewolves, sensing your bravery, accept you into their pack. Together, you roam the forests, wild and free, bound only by the laws of the pack. Though you feel at home in this new life, you are still distant from both the human and magical worlds. This new existence feels like freedom, but you know deep down that you can never fully return to the life you once knew.

Do you fully EMBRACE your life with the pack, destined to live in the shadows forever?
