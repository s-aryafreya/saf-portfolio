This is WHO this is FOR

This story and little adventure is for the fantasy lovers—those who love to dabble in the magical world. Whether you are an avid reader of Lord of the Rings, The Witcher, Twilight, or A Court of Thorns and Roses, this journey is for you.

Do you enjoy Isekai anime? This goes out to you, too! Take the journey into choosing who you want to be and experience what it’s like to make a pivotal decision in a magical realm. You get the choice: remain human or become something otherworldly.

Welcome to the new realm, and be brave, traveler!
