This story and litte adventure is for those fantasy lovers. Those people that love to dabble in the magical world. The avid, Lord of the Rings, Witcher, Twilight, and A Court of Thorns and Roses readers. Take the journey into choosing who you want to be and what it would be like to make a decision in a magical realm. Do you also like isekai animes? Well this one also goes out to you! You get the choice on who you want to be. Human or something otherworldly. Welcome to the new realm, and be brave traveller!
