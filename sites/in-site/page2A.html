Meeting the Vampire

The vampire's cold, pale hand grazes yours as he leads you into the shadows of an alley. His voice, deep and smooth like silk, cuts through the silence. "You have the chance to live forever, to be more than you ever dreamed," he says, his eyes locking onto yours with an intensity that makes your heart race. "All you need to do is accept." His offer lingers in the air, heavy with promise and danger. You feel a pull—eternity calls to you, but so does the freedom to turn away[cite: 5].

ACCEPT the offer and become a vampire

— OR —

REFUSE and run away into the unknown
